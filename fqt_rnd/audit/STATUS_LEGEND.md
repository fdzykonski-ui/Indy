# Evidence status legend

- `VERIFIZIERT`: independently reproduced from immutable input with hashes and
  a machine-readable artifact.
- `TEILWEISE VERIFIZIERT`: direct evidence exists, but a material gate, sample,
  version, or independence condition is incomplete.
- `NICHT VERIFIZIERT`: assertion exists without sufficient reproducible proof.
- `BLOCKIERT`: verification cannot currently be run; the blocker is recorded.

These labels describe evidence quality, not trading profitability.
