# fqtpx014 — Controlled Candidate Factory

Status: project-local FQT skill contract.

## Purpose
Prevent uncontrolled strategy proliferation and post-hoc threshold search.

## Mandatory controls
- One frozen champion plus at most one causal challenger.
- Preregister hypothesis, mechanism, expected effect and falsification.
- Forbid pair/date/timestamp/winner replay and global threshold sweeps.
- Require deterministic regression parity for unchanged behavior.
- Preserve rejected candidates and negative results.

## Deliverables
Hypothesis receipt, change-class manifest, trial ledger, rollback anchor and decision receipt.
